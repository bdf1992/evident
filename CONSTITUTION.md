# Constitution

1. **Sessions are first-class.** Every claim/change is attributable to a bounded session or external source.
2. **Bound before learning.** Every session and claim has scope.
3. **Registration creates coordinates, not truth.**
4. **Predict before interview.** Material pre-interview expectations are frozen so hindsight is visible.
5. **Prediction is not evidence.** It cannot silently become placement or routing authority.
6. **Evidence keeps provenance.** Source, scope, time, perspective, and state survive synthesis.
7. **Perspective is preserved.** Local disagreement is not normalized away for graph cleanliness.
8. **Unknown is information.** Unknown, inaccessible, absent, disputed, and outside-role differ.
9. **Placement is not personality.** It describes evidenced relations in a bounded space.
10. **Knowledge is not authority.** Relevance does not imply ownership, permission, obligation, or approval.
11. **Positive and negative boundaries matter.** Learn where to route and where not to route.
12. **Questions are probes.** Ask to resolve distinctions, not complete a form.
13. **Artifacts and people can challenge each other.** Neither source class is automatically canonical.
14. **Participants can inspect and challenge placement.** Challenges remain attributable evidence.
15. **Thirty minutes is a real investigation budget.** Partial + honest beats complete-looking + late.
16. **Sealed sessions are append-only evidence.** Later sessions may supersede for scope but do not rewrite the old receipt.
17. **Trust is inspectability, not authority theater.** Hashes prove package integrity; provenance and challenges support judgment.
18. **Federation permits inheritance and disagreement.** Parent/local spaces may overlap without becoming identical.
19. **No covert scanning.** Inspect only explicitly allowed sources.
20. **Share minimally.** Prefer references/hashes and redact source content not needed for the session purpose.
