# Evidence Policy

## Atomic rule

Write what the source establishes before writing what it might mean.

Example:

**Documented observation:** `CODEOWNERS` assigns `/billing/` to `@payments-team`.

**Allowed inference:** `@payments-team` may have a review or ownership relation to billing code.

**Not established:** that the team owns the billing product, carries pager duty, approves production changes, or contains the strongest SMEs.

## Evidence states

### registered
A coordinate intentionally supplied to define the space.

### declared
A participant statement. Preserve speaker and scope.

### documented
An artifact contains the proposition or directly supporting content.

### observed
A system state/action directly demonstrates the proposition.

### inferred
The model connects evidence into a conclusion. Cite every input and state uncertainty.

### verified
Multiple sufficiently independent sources agree for the run purpose. This is contextual, not eternal truth.

### contested
A claim has a material challenge or conflicting source.

### unknown
The session did not establish the claim.

## Distinctions that must survive

Do not collapse absent vs not observed, inaccessible vs nonexistent, old vs current, policy vs practice, capability vs authority, participation vs ownership, familiarity vs expertise, relevance vs obligation, parent policy vs local practice, or contradiction vs exception.

## Participant challenge

A participant may challenge any placement or claim. Record target claim, correction, supporting evidence if any, whether the challenge is itself only declared, and resolution status.

## Sensitive evidence

Record only what is necessary for the session purpose. Prefer references/hashes/metadata over copying sensitive contents into the repository.
